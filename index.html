Case Study 01 · T-Bakeville · Food Brand
1.7k → 25k
The Challenge
T-Bakeville had a small, disengaged audience and no clear content identity. Posts were inconsistent and weren't generating real community or buyers.
The Strategy
Built a content identity around founder story, behind-the-scenes kitchen moments, and community-first engagement that made followers feel like insiders — not just customers.
The Result
1,700 → 25,000 Facebook followers in under five months. Zero ad spend. The client messaged: "These are not just ordinary followers — buying ones, engaging ones. And I've not even ran any ad yet."

Case Study 02 · Lynnpastries · Bakery
10k → 45k
The Challenge
Lynnpastries had a decent base but couldn't break through the algorithm or reach Facebook monetization thresholds — limiting their revenue potential.
The Strategy
Introduced viral-friendly content formats — transformation videos, recipe reveals, and relatable bakery-life content — combined with a daily engagement protocol that compounded reach month on month.
The Result
35,000 new followers in 7 months. Engagement up 338%. Views up 170%. Facebook monetization unlocked — opening a brand new revenue stream. Client confirmed: "It's really paying off honestly, got monetized on Facebook, got new clients too."

Case Study 03 · Solar Energy Brand
₦1.5M
The Challenge
A solar brand with under 200 Instagram followers was struggling to attract high-value clients. Their content wasn't speaking to decision-makers or building the trust needed for large purchases.
The Strategy
Crafted a single, precision-targeted Instagram DM using conversion psychology — building credibility fast, addressing the prospect's pain point, and making a confident, clear offer.
The Result
One DM. One reply. ₦1.5 million in revenue. Proof that follower count is never the bottleneck — the right words to the right person always win.
